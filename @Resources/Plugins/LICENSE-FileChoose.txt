FileChoose — third-party Rainmeter plugin (not Rainmeter core)

Author: Setsukka
Version: 1.2.0.1
Forum thread: https://forum.rainmeter.net/viewtopic.php?f=128&t=33767

This skin vendors FileChoose.dll under @Resources/Plugins for Settings
Browse (ChooseFile / ChooseFolder / ChooseImage). FileChoose is a
community plugin distributed via the Rainmeter Forums; it is not part
of the official Rainmeter installation.

The binary shipped here is the 64-bit build corresponding to Ver.1.2.0.1
(sourced from the meters-on-demand/plugins community mirror of the
forum release when direct forum download is Cloudflare-gated).
